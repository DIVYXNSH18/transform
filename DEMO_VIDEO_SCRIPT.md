## 🛠️ Recording Checklist Before You Hit Record

1. **Both Servers Running:**
   - Backend on `http://localhost:8000`
   - Frontend on `http://localhost:3000`
2. **Browser Window Settings:**
   - Chrome or Edge in Fullscreen (`F11` or maximized at 1920×1080).
   - Zoom level at 100% or 90% so all 3 columns of the Fact Graph fit comfortably on screen.
3. **Audio Setup:**
   - Test microphone level and speak clearly.
   - If using the TTS feature in the Video Package, ensure desktop audio is being captured by your recording software so judges can hear the speech synthesis!
4. **Recording Hotkey:**
   - Windows 10/11 built-in: Press `Win + Alt + R` to start/stop recording.
   - Saved videos are found in your `Videos/Captures` folder.
